# 0010 – A synthetic, fictional business mailbox for the AI evaluation

Date: 2026-10-13 · Status: accepted

## Context

The AI evaluation had 49 personal mails (orders as a shopper, job applications, trips). contor is meant to run a
business mailbox: customer and supplier orders, shipments, complaints, escalations, mail that only makes sense with
earlier mail, and patterns that only show across weeks. Real business mail cannot be committed or shared, and a private
corpus is too small and unlabelled to tell whether a prompt change helps.

## Decision

1. **Fictional by construction.** One invented wholesaler (Nordhafen Industriebedarf GmbH) and its partners; only
   `*.example` domains (RFC 2606), phone numbers in a `+cc … 5550 nnnn` pattern, no bank details, a deny list of real
   brands. Deterministic validators enforce this on every build, so the dataset can live in the repository.
2. **Labels first, text second.** A storyline is written as beats with the facts and the labels; the mail text is
   rendered from them and validated against them (every labelled value must be stated in the mail or in a mail it
   depends on, or be marked as derived with the reason). Labels are never reverse-engineered from prose.
3. **Generated by a different model than the one under test.** Claude writes the world, storylines and texts; the
   local model (`qwen3.5:9b`) is only ever evaluated on it. A second, independent labelling pass (a separate model
   context without the storylines) audits the labels; disagreements are resolved by hand and the agreement is recorded.
4. **Sources committed, outputs generated.** `world.json`, `storylines/` and `noise/` are the source; `mailbox/`,
   `expected/` and `manifest.json` are generated by a dependency-free Node script and also committed, so the Java evals
   read plain files. The expected end state is a deterministic fold over the per-mail labels.
5. **Evaluate the pipeline, not only the prompt.** Besides per-mail extraction, triage and importance, a replay pushes
   the whole mailbox through the real worker in time order and compares the end state of every collection (including
   duplicates, e.g. one driver seen through two carriers).

## Consequences

- The dataset grows in slices (4 weeks first) and each slice is reviewed by a human before the next one is written.
- Synthetic mail is cleaner than real mail; the private corpus (`ai-eval/private`) stays the reality check.
- Facts only stated in sent mail are not in the expected state (sent mail is never extracted), which mirrors the app.
- The replay needs Docker and Ollama and takes most of an hour; it is a manual `-Pai-eval` run, not CI.
